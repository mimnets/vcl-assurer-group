## Brand collection (live site, read-only)

- Public pages of `https://www.assurerworld.com/` read in Chrome through computed styles and image
  data. No login, no form, no change. The owner offered WordPress admin access; the agent declined.
- First attempt: the tab moved to the WordPress login page (the owner's own action, to help; the
  browser had autofilled saved credentials). The agent did not log in, closed the tab, discarded
  the values read from that page and asked the owner, who said to try again.
- Logo and favicon PNGs downloaded with `curl` from the public uploads into the agent's scratch
  directory, inspected, then copied or resized into `public/images/brand/`.
- The owner then supplied a flat logo file ("AW Final Logo.png"). It was applied (logo, favicons
  from the mark, primary `#434b74`), shown, and **reverted at the owner's request** in favour of
  the site's textured logo. Its colours are recorded in the token guide, unused.
